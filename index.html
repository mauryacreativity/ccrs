Our Story & Heritage

A Legacy of Dependable Mobility Excellence Since 2014

Classic Car Rental Service Pvt Ltd has emerged as one of the most trusted names in the Indian mobility and corporate transportation industry. What started with a clear vision to simplify travel has grown into a fully operational, GST-registered enterprise with a diversified fleet and an impressive portfolio of corporate clients.

Headquartered in Splendor Tower 2, Golf Course Extension Road, Sector 58, Gurgaon, we provide end-to-end transport management, luxury car rentals, employee commute systems, and outstation taxi services.

Our Mission

To redefine the landscape of car rentals and employee transportation in India by offering safe, dependable, and technology-enabled mobility solutions, ensuring complete transparency, chauffeur discipline, and guest comfort.

Our Vision

To become India’s most admired car rental brand through smart fleet monitoring systems, eco-conscious expansion, transparent pricing, and unmatched customer care across urban and rural centers.
